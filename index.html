About

Who I Am

A driven chemical engineering researcher committed to sustainable innovation and process excellence.

Tejas Ganpat Bhurse is an Integrated Master of Technology candidate in Chemical Engineering at the Institute of Chemical Technology (ICT), Mumbai – IndianOil Odisha Campus, Bhubaneswar, with a minor in Petrochemical Technology and Refinery Engineering. Originally from Gadchiroli district, Maharashtra, he developed an early interest in engineering, scientific problem solving, and sustainable technologies.

His research integrates process systems engineering, Aspen HYSYS simulation, reaction engineering, process optimization, separation processes, renewable energy, and computational modeling. His published and ongoing research includes industrial biodiesel process simulation, methanol recovery optimization, thermal energy storage, carbon capture, and dynamic process modeling. He is also conducting interdisciplinary computational neuroscience research involving neural avalanche propagation using MATLAB.

Research

Full‑Scale Biodiesel Process Synthesis & Methanol Recovery Optimization

Modeled continuous industrial‑scale biodiesel synthesis from high‑FFA waste cooking oil using Aspen HYSYS. Engineered two‑stage catalytic esterification/transesterification flowsheet, atmospheric flash, and parabolic trough solar thermal integration for zero‑carbon solvent recovery.

  • 98.7% FFA conversion · 99.9% TG conversion · 99.9 wt% FAME purity
  • Heat integration: 3.08 → 1.66 MJ/kg (46% reduction)
  • 42.35 t CO₂/yr abated via 136.1 m² PTC array (2.45 yr payback)

Neural Avalanches: Stochastic Sparse Network & Spiking Dynamics

Simulated scale‑free cortical avalanche propagation across 2D/3D synthetic neural sheets in MATLAB. Scaled network eigenvalues to calibrate branching ratio around critical threshold (λ = 0.95), transitioned binary neurons into continuous theta spiking neurons, and rendered 3D spatiotemporal cascade sheets.

  • Power‑law validation: size ~ S⁻¹·⁵, duration ~ T⁻² from 10⁵ cascades
  • Multi‑λ sweep framework across criticality boundary
  • Parallel computing optimization for large‑scale network simulations

Engineering

Techno‑Economic & LCA of VIP in Refrigerator Systems

Evaluated Vacuum Insulated Panels (VIP) replacing conventional polyurethane foam in domestic refrigerators. Modeled fundamental heat transfer (Fourier's law, Knudsen effect), 300L cost‑benefit curves, interior volume gains, BEE regulatory standards, carbon payback (~5 years), and circular recycling roadmaps.

  • Thermal conductivity: 0.004 vs 0.022 W/m‑K (5‑20× improvement)
  • Interior volume: 574L → 749L (30% gain) with 15mm walls
  • Energy savings: 165 kWh/year per unit
